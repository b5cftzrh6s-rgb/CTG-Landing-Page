Definitions & assumptions
  • Volume (gal) = Σ component lb ÷ (component SG × 8.345 lb/gal). Volumes are assumed additive.
  • SG = total lb ÷ (volume × 8.345). Also shown in lb/gal.
  • % Refractory = refractory lb ÷ total lb.
  • % Solids (dry) = (binder lb × binder solids % + polymer lb × polymer solids % + refractory lb) ÷ total lb.
  • % Silica (of liquid) = binder lb × binder solids % ÷ liquid lb (binder + water + polymer). Binder solids are assumed to be silica.
  • % Polymer (of liquid) = (binder lb × binder polymer % + polymer lb × polymer solids %) ÷ liquid lb.
  • % Zircon = zircon lb ÷ total refractory lb. Drives the 7667 dose (linear interpolation of the product-bulletin table, 7.0–10.0 mL/gal of liquid).
  • Antifoam = 2305 mL/L × liquid litres.
  • Polymer SG is an input (default 1.0). Polymer solids default 50 %.
  • Material tables (binder solids/density/polymer, refractory SG): values from product SDS and product information sheets (general-use values).
  • A refractory set to "None" with a weight is ignored and flagged.

Method & assumptions
  • Components: binder as supplied (solids % and SG from the material table), added water (SG 1.000), refractory flour (dry, SG from the table). Volumes are assumed additive (same model as the recipe cards; 8.345 lb/gal water).
  • Adjust: the current tank is split into binder / water / flour by solving three balances: total weight, total volume (weight ÷ SG) and dry solids (binder solids + flour). The target composition comes from target SG and % silica in liquid. The solver finds the smallest additions that reach it; if a component is already in excess, it tells you how much slurry to remove first.
  • Rebuild: target composition × target volume × target SG × 8.345 lb/gal.
  • Binder solids are treated as silica. Polymer in the binder is carried with the binder; separate polymer additions are not solved for. Evaporation, settling and flour wetting are ignored.
  • Viscosity is not predicted. No first-principles viscosity model is used. Make additions in steps and re-measure viscosity (cup or sensor), using the Correlation tool for your site-specific relationship.
  • Results are only as good as the SG and % solids measurements; small errors in % solids change the split noticeably. Re-measure after mixing.
