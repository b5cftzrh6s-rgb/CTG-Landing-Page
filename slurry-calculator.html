Slurry Recipe Calculator

Design and compare up to three slurry recipes, with dosing for 7667 wetting agent and 2305 antifoam.

Definitions & assumptions
  • Volume (gal) = Σ component lb ÷ (component SG × 8.345 lb/gal). Volumes are assumed additive.
  • SG = total lb ÷ (volume × 8.345). Also shown in lb/gal.
  • % Refractory = refractory lb ÷ total lb.
  • % Solids (dry) = (binder lb × binder solids % + polymer lb × polymer solids % + refractory lb) ÷ total lb.
  • % Silica (of liquid) = binder lb × binder solids % ÷ liquid lb (binder + water + polymer). Binder solids are assumed to be silica.
  • % Polymer (of whole slurry) = (binder lb × binder polymer % + polymer lb × polymer solids %) ÷ total slurry lb (binder + water + polymer + refractory). This is the spec basis. % Polymer (of liquid) uses the same numerator ÷ liquid lb and is shown for reference.
  • % Zircon = zircon lb ÷ total refractory lb. Drives the 7667 dose (linear interpolation of the product-bulletin table, 7.0–10.0 mL/gal of liquid).
  • Antifoam = 2305 mL/L × liquid litres.
  • Polymer is fixed at 50 % solids and SG 1.0 (not editable) in all recipe math.
  • Material tables (binder solids/density/polymer, refractory SG): values from product SDS and product information sheets (general-use values).
  • A refractory set to "None" with a weight is ignored and flagged.
