Tank RPM Calculator

Recommended slurry tank rotation speed from tank diameter and target tangential speed (Vp).

Tank inputs

Formula: Vp = π × D × RPM, so RPM = Vp ÷ (π × D), with D in metres (1 in = 0.0254 m).

Presets are CTG rules of thumb, not a published standard. Editing a Vp switches to Custom.

Recommended RPM

Rule of thumb: backup slurries 90–110 m/min; prime 80 m/min (lower speed because more flour may raise particle-attrition risk). Confirm against your tank and drive limits.
